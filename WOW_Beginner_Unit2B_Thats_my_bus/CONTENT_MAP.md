# Content map — Beginner Unit 2B · That’s my bus!

## Lesson scope
- Grammar: Wh- and How questions with `be`.
- Vocabulary: phone numbers; numbers 11–100.
- Pronunciation/listening focus: distinguishing similar numbers such as 13/30, 14/40, etc.
- Functional context: asking for and giving personal information; introducing people; asking how someone is; bus / bus stop goodbye context.

## 7 blocks
1. **Words & Phrases** — 10 meaning checks using the lesson’s core phrases.
2. **Grammar Check** — 10 question-word / word-order items.
3. **Number Detective** — 10 listening items, especially teen vs ty.
4. **Phone & Personal Info** — 8 short real-life dialogue completions.
5. **Reading at the Bus Stop** — original Beginner-level text + 5 questions.
6. **Question Sort** — 9 shuffled cards into PERSON / PLACE / NUMBER.
7. **Listening Mission** — 2 original dialogues + 8 questions.

## Audio chunking
- Vocabulary: one phrase per request.
- Numbers: one number per request.
- Reading: 8 short chunks.
- Listening: one dialogue line per request; phone number line is split into three smaller chunks.
