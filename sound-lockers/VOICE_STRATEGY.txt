VOICE STRATEGY — SOUND LOCKERS v3

THIS VERSION
The exercise does NOT use browser SpeechSynthesis and does NOT generate a robotic voice at runtime.
Vocabulary audio is played from real human British-English pronunciation recordings hosted by
Wikimedia Commons / the Shtooka Project.

The Shtooka files used for the core model examples identify a female speaker from London, England
(Judith Franck) and are licensed for reuse under CC BY 3.0 US. See AUDIO_SOURCES.txt.

WHY THIS APPROACH FOR SINGLE WORDS
For isolated phonics vocabulary, a verified human pronunciation is preferable to synthesizing
each word on the learner's device. It gives stable stress, vowel quality and British pronunciation
without depending on whatever voice is installed in Chrome/Windows.

FOR FUTURE SENTENCES / DIALOGUES
Recommended premium generation path:
1. ElevenLabs Eleven v3 — highest-fidelity / most expressive ElevenLabs model.
2. Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD with English (United Kingdom), en-GB — strong alternative.

Those services require an account/API key to generate and store bespoke audio.
For this ready-to-upload version no API key is required.
