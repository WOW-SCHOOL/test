# WOW SCHOOL — English File Beginner — 1&2 Revise and Check

Interactive review module built on the current WOW SCHOOL 16:9 template.

## Coverage
- File 1: verb **be** (I / you / he / she / it), numbers 0–10, days, countries, classroom language, alphabet / spelling, Practical English Episode 1.
- File 2: verb **be** (we / you / they), nationalities, Wh- and How questions with **be**, numbers 11–100, phone numbers and personal information.
- Review skills: vocabulary, grammar, listening for numbers / personal information, real-life English, reading, pronunciation and two new listening dialogues.

## Structure
1. Vocabulary Mix — 12 tasks
2. Grammar Review — 12 tasks
3. Numbers & Personal Info — 10 listening tasks
4. Real-life English — 10 tasks
5. Reading Mission — 6 questions
6. Pronunciation Sort — 12 cards (/iː/, /əʊ/, /ʃ/)
7. Listening Mission — 2 dialogues, 8 questions
8. Results

## Audio
The existing WOW SCHOOL British-English synthesis mechanism is preserved. Long passages and dialogues are played as short sequential chunks; no local WAV replacement is used.

## Images
Final generated visuals are intentionally postponed to the next step by user request. Planned filenames and safe-use notes are in `VISUAL_MANIFEST.json`. The current build uses the standard WOW SCHOOL fallback panels until those files are added.
