# WOW SCHOOL · English File Beginner · Unit 1B Review

Тема: **World music**.

## Материал урока
- Grammar: `verb be` (singular) — `he / she / it`; affirmative, negative, yes/no questions and short answers.
- Vocabulary: 15 core countries from Lesson 1B; core phrases `Where are you from?`, `Where’s…?`, `I think it’s in…`, `I don’t know`, `Very good`, `Wow!`.
- Pronunciation: `/ɪ/`, `/əʊ/`, `/s/`, `/ʃ/`; distinction between `he` and `she` in listening.
- Communication: ask where a person is from / where a place is, and answer with `He’s / She’s / It’s…` and short `Yes / No` answers.

## 7 blocks
1. **Countries & Phrases** — 10 tasks: countries + useful phrases.
2. **Grammar Check** — 10 tasks: `he / she / it + be`.
3. **Sound Detective** — 10 listening/pronunciation tasks on the four Lesson 1B sounds.
4. **Where from?** — 8 contextual mini-dialogue responses.
5. **Reading · World Music Chat** — new Beginner-level dialogue + 5 comprehension questions.
6. **He · She · It Sort** — 10 drag-and-drop cards in exactly 3 categories (`HE`, `SHE`, `IT`). Drag-and-drop and click → column both work.
7. **Listening Mission** — 2 new mini-dialogues + 8 comprehension questions.

## Visuals
The module uses newly generated realistic, bright photo scenes for the preview/hero, countries, pronunciation, contextual dialogue, reading, and listening. Every photo is clipped by the actual inner visual container; `overflow:hidden` and `object-fit` prevent images from leaving the grey/soft frame.
